Heater shutdown

Each cycle, a controller reads two temperature sensors and sends the heater an ON or OFF command. What happens when the temperature reaches its safety limit?

Shutdown limit

80 °C

If either reading reaches this limit or is invalid, the output must be OFF by the end of that same cycle.
