The four safety requirements

Both readings must be valid and below 80 °C to count as safe. Each rule applies by the end of the current cycle.

  1. A1 · Shut down. If either reading is unsafe, engage the lock and turn the heater off, even during reset.
  2. A2 · Hold the lock. If the cycle starts locked and reset is inactive, keep the lock engaged and the heater off.
  3. A3 · Release the lock. When both readings are safe and reset is active, clear the lock.
  4. A4 · Follow the heat request. With safe readings and a lock that was clear or is being reset, follow the heat request. Whenever heat is not requested, keep the heater off.

These four rules are written in ACSL, the specification language Frama-C reads. Read the contract in controller.h
